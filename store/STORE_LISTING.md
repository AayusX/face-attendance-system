## Long description

Face Attendance System is a lightweight, fully offline attendance app for schools,
colleges, and small organisations. It runs entirely on your PC — no cloud, no
account, no external services.

Features:
- Automatic face-recognition attendance from your webcam
- Duplicate-registration guard so each face is registered once
- Register, rename, and delete students
- Live attendance table and session controls
- Dashboard with present/absent counts and a last-7-days trend
- Reports with date range, text search, and Present/Absent filters
- Full Nepali (Bikram Sambat) calendar support — dates shown in BS, and date
  inputs accept both AD (e.g. 2026-09-17) and BS (e.g. 2083-06-01)
- Export reports to CSV or Excel with a "date (BS)" column
- Light, clean UI; admin login (customisable)

Privacy: face data is stored only on your device as numerical embeddings. Nothing
is uploaded anywhere. See the privacy policy link for details.
